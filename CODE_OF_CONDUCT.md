# Code of Conduct

OpenWatch follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, no harassment, no personal attacks, and no publishing of
anyone's private information. Political discussion of the data is fine; abuse of people is not.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via GitHub (@d-rohan-web). Reports are handled confidentially,
and maintainers may remove content or ban participants who break these rules.
