# Notice: purpose and acceptable use

**OpenWatch India is an independent, non-commercial project for study, research and general public awareness.**

- It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built for** any military, defence, intelligence, police or government
  body, in India or elsewhere. No military organisation is involved in it.
- It shows **only publicly available information** (open APIs, public government pages, open datasets such as
  OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, Natural Earth). It contains no classified, leaked or restricted material.
- Data can be **delayed, incomplete or wrong**. OpenWatch is **not** suitable for navigation, aviation, maritime,
  emergency-response, safety-of-life, military or any other operational decision-making. Always rely on the
  official source (IMD, NDMA, INCOIS, AAI, the Indian Navy's NAVAREA VIII service, etc.).
- **Please don't use OpenWatch for military, targeting, surveillance of individuals or any unlawful purpose.**
  Contributions intended for such uses will not be accepted.
- The map shows India's boundary as per the Survey of India. Optional de-facto lines are labelled as such and
  imply no position.
